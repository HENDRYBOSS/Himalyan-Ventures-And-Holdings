About Us

Himalayan Ventures and Holdings Pvt. Ltd. is a Nepal-based investment and trading company dedicated to building long-term value through disciplined capital allocation across key economic sectors. Rooted in the resilience and strength symbolized by the Himalayas, the company operates with a clear and focused mandate: to invest and trade exclusively in the share market, commodity market, real estate, and small and medium enterprises (SMEs).

With a strategic emphasis on diversification and risk-managed growth, the company creates sustainable financial returns while contributing meaningfully to Nepal's evolving economic landscape. Operating from Kathmandu, it aligns activities with capital formation, efficient markets, and enterprise development — integrating careful market analysis, prudent risk assessment, and disciplined execution.

Core Business Areas

📊 Share Market

Balanced mix of long-term equity investments and tactical trading. Emphasis on portfolio diversification across sectors to reduce concentration risk, targeting fundamentally sound companies with stable earnings and credible management.

🏅 Commodity Market

Metals and natural commodities: strategic positioning based on price trends, macro factors, and risk-managed exposure. The company leverages commodity volatility as an investment opportunity while maintaining disciplined controls.

🏢 Real Estate

Investment and trading focused on long-term capital appreciation, location advantage, and market demand. Assets selected for strong potential and periodic liquidity events to optimize returns.

🏭 Small & Medium Enterprises

Structured investments (equity, debentures) in scalable SMEs with proven business models. Goal: enterprise development, job creation, and alignment with Nepal’s growth trajectory.
